Pilot instructions

Use the real app for the file handoff. These panels replay sample states for comparing the mascot and the optional CLI row.

  1. Give each of five Mac-and-Linux users the pilot file. Once SSH is ready, ask them to send it and find the delivered copy.
  2. In a disposable setup, interrupt a send and ask them to recover without coaching. Record whether they identified the destination and found Retry.
  3. Show both previews in alternating order. Ask who needs attention in the second preview and whether they can name a recurring workflow with more than one active CLI.

Keep the motion if four of five understand the transfer states without help. Advance the CLI row if at least two identify the correct session and can name a recurring use for it.
